🌀 Rip Current Risk

—

Caught in a rip current?
  • Don't fight it. You can't out-swim a rip.
  • Stay calm and float to keep your head above water.
  • Swim parallel to the shore to escape the current, then angle back in.
  • Can't escape? Float, wave, and yell for help.

🐚 Good to know on the Gulf

⚡ Lightning

If you hear thunder, you're close enough to be struck. Leave the water and get indoors. Wait 30 minutes after the last thunder before returning.

🪼 Jellyfish & Man o' War

Common in warm Gulf water, especially with onshore winds. Rinse stings with vinegar or seawater (not fresh water), remove tentacles, and seek help for severe reactions.

🦈 Stingrays

They rest in shallow sand. Do the "stingray shuffle" — slide your feet rather than stepping down — to scare them off.

🌡️ Heat & hydration

Gulf humidity makes heat feel worse. Drink water often, take shade breaks, and watch kids and older adults for heat exhaustion.

🩴 Hot sand & sun

Reapply reef-safe SPF every 2 hours and after swimming. Sand can burn bare feet by midday.

🚩 Swim near lifeguards

Most drownings happen on unguarded beaches. Know the flag color before you get in, and never swim alone.
