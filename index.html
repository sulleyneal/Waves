Caught in a rip current?
  • Don't fight it. You can't out-swim a rip.
  • Stay calm and float to keep your head above water.
  • Swim parallel to shore to escape, then angle back in.
  • Can't escape? Float, wave, and yell for help.

🐚 Good to know on the Gulf

⚡ Lightning

If you hear thunder, you're close enough to be struck. Leave the water and get indoors. Wait 30 minutes after the last thunder.

🪼 Jellyfish & Man o' War

Common in warm Gulf water with onshore winds. Rinse stings with vinegar or seawater (not fresh water) and remove tentacles.

🦈 Stingrays

They rest in shallow sand. Do the "stingray shuffle" — slide your feet rather than stepping down.

🌡️ Heat & hydration

Gulf humidity makes heat feel worse. Drink water often and take shade breaks.

🩴 Sun & sand

Reapply reef-safe SPF every 2 hours and after swimming. Sand burns bare feet by midday.

🚩 Swim near lifeguards

Most drownings happen on unguarded beaches. Know the flag color and never swim alone.
